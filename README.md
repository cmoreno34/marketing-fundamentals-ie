# Marketing Fundamentals — in-class case tools

Browser-based case workspaces for **Marketing Fundamentals** (IE University, BBA year 1).
Everything runs in the student's browser. No account, no server, no data leaves the machine.

| Tool | Session | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [Cold Chain Decision](./tomato-case/) | 22 | Tomato preservation case: size the spoilage prize, read the industry, commit to one route to market. Arithmetic is auto-checked; each step generates an AI prompt the student uses as a sparring partner. |
| [Positioning Maps](./positioning-maps/) | 5 | Upload brand scores (CSV or the survey workbook), pick the brand you are positioning, and read every positioning map: closest competitor, distance to the market mean, the four most telling maps. Conclusions export to PDF; AI help comes as prompts, guidance first and refinement once the answers are written. |
| [Sariwa Silk](./sariwa-silk/) | 6 | Short homework on the Quick Case: the exhibit figures are built in and every calculation is done by the page; four short parts (launch, target and positioning, 2024 targets) with 13 click-or-one-line questions, exported as a PDF. The case text is not reproduced. |
