Session 12 · questionnaire design

Survey architect

You write the brief; the page writes the prompt. Every rule of the session travels inside it: 1 to 5 scales wherever the question can take one, one idea per question, a screener at the start, demographics at the end, and an analysis plan for every question you ask.

The decision, first

If you cannot say what you would do differently depending on the answer, the survey is not worth running. Write that first.

Demographics you will cross the answers by

Only the ones you will actually use in a table. Every extra one costs you answers.

Blocks to include

The analysis plan this brief implies

Written before the questionnaire, as the session asks. Each row is a variable you will have in your dataset and the table it ends up in.

Your prompt

Copy it into whichever assistant you use. Read what comes back with the checklist in the next tab: the questionnaire is yours, not the model's.

Fill in the brief and press "Write my prompt".
Before you send it to anybody: a questionnaire written by a model is a draft. It will be too long, it will repeat itself, and one question in five will be leading. Cut it to the number you asked for and rewrite the ones the checklist catches.

Review what it wrote

Go through this with the draft in front of you. Anything you cannot tick is a question to rewrite, not a detail.
