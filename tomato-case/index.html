The brief and the evidence

Read first

The technology. A patented refrigerated container that controls humidity and holds −4 °C without freezing the load. Fresh tomatoes stay saleable past six months.

The choice. The team is split three ways and cannot fund more than one.

A · License
Sell the IP to reefer makers — CIMC, Singamas, Carrier. Royalties, no factory.
B · Manufacture
Build the container and sell it to logistics firms. Own the margin, own the capex.
C · Ship tomatoes
Run an international tomato delivery service using your own containers.

Exhibit 3 · What is moving in the industry
  • Red Sea rerouting adds roughly 10–14 days to affected voyages. Longer voyage, more spoilage.
  • IMO decarbonisation rules (EEXI / CII) push carriers towards energy-efficient reefer units.
  • Container makers: CIMC, Singamas. Refrigeration units: Star Cool, Thermo King, Carrier Transicold.
  • Buyers are concentrated in every model — a handful of OEMs, a handful of shipping lines, a handful of retail importers.
