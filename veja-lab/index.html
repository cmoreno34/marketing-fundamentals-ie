The survey

A group of students designed and ran this survey for VAJA, a carbonated drink built around birch sap: allergy relief, immune boost and relaxation without caffeine. They asked 18 rating questions on a 1 to 5 scale and 7 demographic questions, and got 77 complete answers.

That is a small sample and a self-selected one: everything you find here is a hypothesis about the market, not a measurement of it. It is still enough to practise every analysis of the session, and to see what a real dataset looks like when it arrives.

The codebook

Every analysis starts here: what was asked, how it was measured, and what each number means. Without it, a column of 1s and 5s says nothing.

The data clinic

Describe: one variable at a time

For a 1 to 5 rating: the mean, how spread the answers are, and the share who answered 4 or 5 — the top-two box, which is what a client reads. For a category: the frequency table.

Categories

Cross: does the answer depend on the group?

Two categorical variables. Row percentages, expected counts, chi-square, and the validity check before the p-value. To cross a rating, the lab bands it into low (1-2), middle (3) and high (4-5).

Segment: k-means on the ratings

The 18 rating questions describe what each respondent wants. K-means groups the people whose answers look alike, and the centroid table tells you what each group is. Choose how many segments and read the table, not the algorithm.
