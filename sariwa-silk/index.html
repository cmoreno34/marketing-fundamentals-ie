The brief. B&T Goods International wants to launch Sariwa Silk shampoo in a 100 g tube, sold for travel and precise dosing. The Sariwa Silk line grew 6% in 2023 and B&T wants 15% growth in 2024. Sid, the president, expects the tube to bring new customers. Rex, the head of marketing, fears existing buyers will simply switch from the bottles.
