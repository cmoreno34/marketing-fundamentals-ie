The brief. B&T Goods International, a premium personal-care company, plans to launch Sariwa Silk shampoo in a 100 g tube in the Philippines, first of its kind in the market, sold for precise dosing and travel. The Sariwa Silk line grew 6% in 2023 and B&T wants 15% growth in 2024 with the launch. Sid Mendoza, the president, is convinced the tube will bring new customers. Rex Lim, the head of marketing, is not: he thinks existing buyers may switch from the bottles, and he wants to know whether the target should be the same in Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao.

Your job: decide whether B&T should launch the tube, for whom and positioned against what, and what the 2024 sales targets should be, region by region, in value and in volume.

How this page works
